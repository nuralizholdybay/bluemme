Grammar Rule #1

Avoid Present Continuous for Habits

Incorrect: "I am helping my host mom every evening."
Correct: "I help my host mom every evening." or "I helped my host mom..."

Grammar Rule #2

Specific Time = Past Simple

Incorrect: "I have joined the student council last year."
Correct: "I joined the student council last year."
